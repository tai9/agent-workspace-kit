# Product strategy

The document every product-owner verdict is an application of. Until
adopted, product-owner cannot issue a GO — "no strategy doc exists" is
its first finding. Adoption interviews the owner; this file records
answers, it does not invent them.

## North star
The one metric or outcome the product optimizes, and what it deliberately
does not optimize.

## ICP — who we build for
The specific user (not a demographic blur), what they struggle with, and
who we accept losing.

## Moat
The bet competitors can't easily copy, and what would falsify it.

## Hard constraints
Budget, team, platform, COGS realities — the walls verdicts must respect.

## Standing risks
Named risks to weigh (not auto-veto) in every verdict, each with the
sign it is materializing.
